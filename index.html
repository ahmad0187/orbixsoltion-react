(06) FAQ

Questions we always get.

Will customers know they're talking to an AI?

Yes, and that's a good thing. Under the EU AI Act, people should know when they're speaking with an AI. Our agents say so naturally, sound warm and human, and pass the conversation to your team whenever a person is needed.

How much does a project cost?

Every project gets a fixed quote in writing after a free discovery call, so there are no surprises. AI agents usually start as a fixed-price pilot for one use case, and their running costs (AI, phone minutes) are typically a few cents per conversation.

How long does a website or app take?

A marketing website usually takes 2 to 4 weeks, an AI chatbot or voice agent 1 to 3 weeks, and a web or mobile app 6 to 12 weeks depending on features. You get a clear timeline before we start.

Which AI models do you use?

Whatever fits the job and your budget: OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini or European models like Mistral. We're not tied to one vendor, so we can switch if a better or cheaper model appears.

Is our data safe and GDPR compliant?

Yes. We design for GDPR and similar privacy laws from day one: data hosted in your region where available, data processing agreements, access controls and no training on your data.

Can it connect to the tools we already use?

Almost certainly. We connect agents to calendars, CRMs (HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce), Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, WhatsApp, phone systems, Slack and custom databases.

Do you build websites and apps without AI?

Absolutely. Websites, WordPress and e-commerce stores, web apps and mobile apps are a big part of what we do. AI is there when it helps, never forced in.
